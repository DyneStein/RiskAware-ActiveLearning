HAM10000
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Some files here are derived from the HAM10000 dataset, which belongs to its
authors and is licensed CC BY-NC (attribution required, non-commercial use
only). Those files remain under that licence and are not relicensed by anything
in this repository:

    Oracle_Simulated_Doctor/MetaData of Dataset (not seed data).csv
    Seed Data/seed_metadata.csv

Both are label tables -- image identifiers and their diagnoses. They are
included because the code cannot reproduce anything without knowing which image
has which diagnosis.

The HAM10000 IMAGES are not redistributed here at all. Download them from the
original source and point DATA_ROOT at your copy. See HOW_TO_RUN.txt.

Required attribution:

    Tschandl, P., Rosendahl, C. & Kittler, H. The HAM10000 dataset, a large
    collection of multi-source dermatoscopic images of common pigmented skin
    lesions. Scientific Data 5, 180161 (2018).
    https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/DBW86T
